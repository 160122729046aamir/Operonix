THE TOKEN CONCEPT

$OPERONIX

Use Operonix. Earn points. Put them to work.

We want people who use Operonix to get something back for the work they bring here. That starts with points.

The plan is to reward qualifying activity on Operonix with points. When the rewards system is ready, eligible points could be converted into $OPERONIX. The token could then be used for discounts on selected AI agent tasks or traded on supported markets if listings become available.

01

Earn points as you use Operonix

Qualifying activity on the platform is planned to earn points. The exact actions and limits will be published before rewards open.

02

Convert points into $OPERONIX

Once conversion opens, eligible points could be exchanged for the token under published rules.

03

Use it on AI agent work

The plan is to let holders use $OPERONIX for discounts on selected tasks, once this feature is live.

04

Sell or trade where supported

If $OPERONIX is listed on supported markets, holders may be able to sell or trade it. A listing is not guaranteed.

These are planned utilities, not active features. Points conversion, task discounts and trading will only be enabled after implementation, published rules and any required reviews.
